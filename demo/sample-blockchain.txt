BLOCKCHAIN TECHNOLOGY: A COMPREHENSIVE OVERVIEW

Introduction to Blockchain
Blockchain is a distributed ledger technology that maintains a continuously growing list of records, called blocks, which are linked and secured using cryptography. Each block contains a cryptographic hash of the previous block, a timestamp, and transaction data.

Core Concepts
1. Decentralization - No single point of control
2. Transparency - All transactions are visible to network participants
3. Immutability - Once recorded, data cannot be altered retroactively
4. Consensus Mechanisms - Agreement protocols like Proof of Work and Proof of Stake

Applications
- Cryptocurrency (Bitcoin, Ethereum)
- Smart Contracts
- Supply Chain Management
- Healthcare Records
- Digital Identity Verification

Advantages
- Enhanced security through cryptography
- Reduced costs by eliminating intermediaries
- Improved traceability
- Increased efficiency and speed

Challenges
- Scalability issues
- Energy consumption (especially Proof of Work)
- Regulatory uncertainty
- Integration with existing systems
